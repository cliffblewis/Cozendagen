About & Press

Meet the person behind the pseudo-Swedish word.

[ PHOTO — Cliff Lewis ]

Cliff Lewis

Cliff Lewis is a Lancaster-based creative director, storyteller, and brand strategist — the author of the novel We the Future, and the first person to be named Lancaster's best storyteller by the Lancaster Story Slam.

Cozendagen began as a private family tradition roughly a decade ago and became a public event in Lancaster in 2026. Cliff now leads its growth as a cultural tradition across Pennsylvania, working city by city to find each town's version of West Art.

FAQ
How do you pronounce Cozendagen?

Coze-en-doggin. We made it up — a pseudo-Swedish word that, according to us, means "cozy days."

Is Cozendagen a Christmas or holiday-season event?

No — deliberately not. Cozendagen begins at the winter solstice (or after New Year's, if you're still deep in Christmas mode) and runs through the spring equinox. It's a nondenominational season built around winter itself, not the December holidays.

Do I need to attend an official event to celebrate?

Not at all. Cozendagen is mostly a private, personal tradition — soup, candlelight, warm socks, a quiet Saturday. Public events like the West Art festival are just one way to celebrate it together.

How much does it cost to attend an event?

Our flagship events are free to attend, with an RSVP requested so hosts can plan food and seating. Some community-hosted events (like film screenings) may have their own ticket price.

My venue wants to host — what's the process?

Start with the Host a Cozendagen Event page and fill out the application. We personally review every submission and follow up directly — no automated screening.

Where does the clothing drive donate to?

Each host venue partners with a shelter local to its own city. In Lancaster, donations from the 2026 festival went directly to shelters serving the local winter population.
