Photography Treatments

An exploration of how event photography lives within the Cozendagen design system. Each treatment below is a reusable pattern for the website.

Vignette

An inset shadow that darkens the edges and draws the eye inward. Subtle but effective for hero-adjacent moments where you want the photo to feel intimate and focused.

Photo + Folk Circle Accent

A photo with a folk-icon circle overlapping the edge — bridging the illustrated and photographic worlds. The circle anchors a thematic icon to the photo content.

Design system note: All treatments above use the Cozendagen palette and type system. Photography rules, once finalised, will be documented in the styleguide alongside icon placement and band layout conventions.
