# Cozendagen — UX & Information Architecture Brief

Derived from a discovery interview with Cliff Lewis, September 2026. This captures
decisions about audience, objectives, and site structure. Where the content handoff
doc (`CONTENTHANDOFF.md`) conflicts with this file, this file is newer and wins.

---

## 1. Strategic context

**Ambition:** Lancaster holiday → Pennsylvania holiday → Northeast → national.

**Current framing (2026–27 season):** a Lancaster holiday that is *becoming* a
Pennsylvania one. Every town being approached this cycle is in PA. "Northeast" and
"national" are later-year narratives and should not be claimed yet — the exception
is the host-your-own-party track, which is deliberately open to anyone anywhere.

**Timeline, worked backward from the goal:**

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| News coverage lands | Late Dec 2026 / early Jan 2027 |
| Pitch regional monthlies (2–3 mo. lead) | Mid-October 2026 |
| Pitch weeklies and dailies | Early December 2026 |
| **Site must be outreach-credible** | **Mid-October 2026** |
| Vendor outreach → passport design → print | Starts immediately |

Vendor outreach is the most time-critical chain: vendors must be selected before the
passport can be designed, designed before printed, printed before the season opens.

---

## 2. Audience priority

The website's most important audiences are **people Cliff approaches outbound**, not
people who discover the site organically. This is the central insight of the
interview and it changes the site's job.

### Primary — the site does *corroboration*, not conversion

1. **Local vendors** — sponsorship and Cozendagen passport participation
2. **Venue owners in other PA towns** — hosting their own events
3. **Press** — regional Pennsylvania publications

The pitch itself lives in one-sheet PDFs and personal conversation (in-person, email,
or social DM), and is deliberately kept off the public site so outreach stays
selective. These audiences arrive already primed, having just spoken with Cliff. They
are checking whether this is real, substantial, and worth attaching their name to.

### Secondary — accommodated, not optimized for

4. **Event attendees and general public** — most communication reaches them via social
   media, and ticketing often happens on host venues' own sites. What they need from
   this site is a *centralized event hub* (because events are scattered across venues)
   and a way to join the email list.
5. **Individuals anywhere who want to host their own gathering** — the one track with
   deliberate national reach. If a post goes viral, this is how someone in another
   state participates.

---

## 3. The unifying message

> **This exploded in winter 2026, and 2027 is going to be bigger.**

This is the homepage's first-priority job. It is the rare message that serves every
audience simultaneously:

- **Vendors** hear earning potential
- **Venue owners** hear proven model
- **Attendees** hear FOMO and social proof
- **Press** hear a trend story

Everything else on the homepage is secondary to establishing this.

## 4. Evidence inventory

Real and usable today:

- West Art repeatedly expanded the space dedicated to the event and kept selling out
- It was West Art's biggest night on record at the time
- The soup vendor sold out
- The Zoetropolis movie night sold out
- 100+ guests at the inaugural festival
- LNP / LancasterOnline editorial
- The clothing drive collected a large volume at West Art

**Still to gather:** testimonial quotes from West Art, Zoetropolis, the soup vendor,
and the bakery. These are high priority — peers vouching for the *commercial*
experience of participating is the single most persuasive asset for the vendor and
venue audiences.

**Framing note:** "every participating business sold out, and the host venue had its
biggest night on record" reframes sponsorship from charitable support to commercial
upside. That should be load-bearing, not buried.

**Do not use:** the three community quotes in the content handoff doc
("Wow. How did I miss this?!" etc.) are placeholder copy, not real reactions, despite
the doc labeling them as real and "shared with permission."

## 5. Sitemap

Six live pages plus one Phase 2. Down from eight.

| Page | Job |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Establish momentum. Succinctly define the holiday. Route to events, press, email. |
| **The Story** | The full case for the holiday. Source material for the press narrative. |
| **How to Celebrate** | Traditions and inspiration, *plus* the host-your-own-party kit and email signup. The national on-ramp. |
| **Events** | Centralized hub across venues, linking out to each venue's own ticketing. |
| **Press** | Functional newsroom: quick facts, publishable photos, prior coverage, contact. |
| **About** | Cliff, the founding, how to reach him. |
| **Passport** *(Phase 2)* | Consumer-facing explainer. Ships after vendors are selected and the booklet is designed. |

### Cut

- **Host an Event** (venue application) — invited exactly the inbound volume Cliff
  doesn't want. Venue recruitment is hand-picked and happens offline.
- **Clothing Drive** — the drive is a collection bin at events plus social promotion
  beforehand. It doesn't need a page; a homepage promo band and a line in each event
  listing covers it.
- **Community** — its best content (photos, reactions) is momentum evidence that
  belongs on the homepage. Nothing load-bearing remains once that moves up.

Reducing eight nav items to six also resolves the header crowding structurally rather
than cosmetically.

## 6. Homepage structure

Priority order per Cliff, with additions noted:

1. **Hero** — the holiday, with the momentum hook
2. **Momentum proof** — sellouts, capacity expansions, biggest night on record.
   *Replaces the current stat strip, which leads with "1 city so far" — the wrong
   number to set in display type on a page whose job is to convey explosive growth.*
3. **What Cozendagen is** — succinct, with a click-through to The Story
4. **Events** — what's next, linking to the hub
5. **Press coverage** *(added)* — third-party validation does credibility work that
   self-description cannot, for every audience
6. **Partner testimonials** *(added)* — once gathered; the vendor-facing proof
7. **Passport promo** — Phase 2 slot
8. **Clothing drive promo** — brief
9. **Email capture** *(added)* — for a seasonal holiday this is the whole retention
   mechanism. There is currently nowhere on the site to give Cliff an address.
10. **Footer**

## 7. Email strategy

Two streams, segmented at capture:

- **General list** — seasonal and event updates; brings people back next November
- **Host-your-own-party track** — a resource sequence helping someone get their first
  private gathering off the ground

The second is the value exchange that makes signup worth it, and the mechanism by
which someone outside Pennsylvania can participate.
