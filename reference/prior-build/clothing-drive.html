The Clothing Drive

When we say "celebrate coziness," we mean coziness for all.

A holiday built around warmth doesn't get to stop at the door. Every Cozendagen event doubles as a collection point for winter clothing, donated to shelters in the city that's hosting.

What to bring
🧤

Gloves

New or gently used, any size.

🧣

Winter Hats

The kind you'd actually want to wear.

🧦

Long, Warm Socks

Consistently the most requested item at local shelters.

Bring your donation to any Cozendagen event and drop it at the collection point near the entrance — no RSVP or purchase required to give.

Why it matters

The whole point of Cozendagen is warmth that's earned against the cold — not decoration laid on top of it.

That idea doesn't mean much if it only shows up as string lights and hot cocoa for people who already have a warm place to go home to. The clothing drive is how the season stays honest: real warmth, passed along to neighbors who need it most, alongside the soup and the candlelight.

2026 recap

Thanks to everyone who brought hats, gloves, and socks to the inaugural Cozendagen Winter Festival at West Art — every donation went directly to shelters serving Lancaster's winter population. As Cozendagen grows to new cities, each host venue will partner with a local shelter of its own.
