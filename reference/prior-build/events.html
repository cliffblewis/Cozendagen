Cozendagen Day is any day. These are the ones worth marking.

The Cozendagen season runs all winter long — but especially when it snows. Below: our flagship Lancaster events, community-hosted gatherings, and where we're headed next.

The Cozendagen Winter Festival at West Art

5:30–10 p.m. at West Art's Sanctuary, transformed into the Cozendagen Ski Lodge. Hot soup, hot drinks, hot dessert, live music, comfy seating, and a proper clothing drive at the door. Free admission — RSVP so we book enough soup vendors.
