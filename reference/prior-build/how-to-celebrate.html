How to Celebrate

If you've ever reveled in any of the following, you've celebrated Cozendagen without knowing it.

Cozendagen has no rulebook and no shopping list. It's a frame for things you probably already do — practiced on purpose, so they don't just happen at random.

The essentials
🏠

Home

A fully lit house on a dark evening. Staying in on purpose, not by default.

🔥

Hearth

A fireplace, a wood stove, or just a candle doing the work of a fire.

🏕

Bonfire Hangs

Cold air, warm flames, and people who stay outside a little longer for it.

🧦

Warm Socks

The unglamorous MVP of the entire season.

☕

Hot Drinks & Soup

Tea, cocoa, cider, or a proper simmering pot. The centerpiece of every gathering.

🧶

Fuzzy Sweaters

Worn without irony. Bonus points for hand-knit.

🪡

Comfy Crafts

Knitting, sewing, puzzles — anything with your hands and nowhere to be.

📖

Cozy Mysteries

A book and a blanket, ideally at the same time.

🕯

Candlelight

Low, warm light after dark — the closest thing Cozendagen has to a mascot.

It's all about setting an intention

Why call it "Cozendagen" if we're already doing this stuff anyway? Proper nouns and rituals help us prioritize the things modern life pushes to the margins. So we call our winter comforts "Cozendagen" and lean into them with conviction — like we're kindling a still-delicate flame.

A few ways to observe it
Solo

A daylong date with a book and a blanket

No agenda beyond staying warm and unbothered. Put the phone away, put the soup on, and let a Saturday be quiet on purpose.

Small

A Cozendagen party with your favorite friends

Ski-lodge energy in a normal living room. Ask everyone to bring a soup, a sweater, or a story to tell by candlelight.

Family

A family hang, low-pressure by design

No gifts, no performance — just the people you love, gathered close, doing the low-key things that actually feel good in January.

Which soup sign are you?

There are more than three kinds of soup, and more than three kinds of people.

But there's room in these three for every personality — chowder people, broth purists, and everyone who just wants something orange and warm in a bowl. Come find out which one you are at the next Cozendagen event.
