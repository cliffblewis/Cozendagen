A deep winter holiday season · Born in Lancaster, PA

We deserve a winter that actually gets us through the winter.

Cozendagen is a nondenominational holiday season celebrating the simple joy of coziness — soup, candlelight, warm socks, and each other. It runs from the winter solstice to the spring equinox. Our first public season sold out every night we held one. This year we're going bigger.

So what is it, exactly?

A holiday about staying in, done on purpose.

Winter as environment

Not a theme laid over the cold — a way of living inside it. Coziness you earn against the weather, not decoration you hang despite it.

Simple, low-pressure ritual

Soup. Candlelight. A blanket and a good book, or a room full of friends. No gifts to buy, no calendar to keep.

Warmth, shared

Every gathering carries forward a winter clothing drive, so the season's warmth reaches neighbors who need it most.

Lancaster, PA

It started with one family shaking off the winter blues.

Ten years ago, Cozendagen began as one Lancaster family's answer to the January slump — the long, gray stretch after the holiday lights come down. Last winter it became a public tradition: West Art's Sanctuary transformed into the Cozendagen Ski Lodge, with hot soup, hot drinks, hot dessert, and a room full of neighbors in fuzzy sweaters.

West Art is the model for what a Cozendagen venue looks like — a space that can own a piece of the night and already has a community around it that shows up for something new.
